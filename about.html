Who We Are

WORLD OF FRAGRANCE is a perfume store serving customers looking for Indian and imported perfumes and attars, with wholesale and retail options.

Whether you walk in for a single attar vial or enquire for wholesale quantities, you are guided the same way — smell first, understand the character, then decide.

  • Indian attars for lovers of rich, traditional fragrance character.
  • Imported-style perfumes for clean, modern, premium profiles.
  • Wholesale & retail — for personal use and business needs.
  • Customer-first exchange & refund assurance. Terms on request.
